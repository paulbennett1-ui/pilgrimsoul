Fellow Travellers

The path is walked
together

A place to leave a word, start a thread, and find others who feel the pull of old roads and open country.

The gathering place

Every pilgrim's hostel has a guestbook. Every waymarked path has stones left by those who came before. This is that place.

Leave a word — where you are walking, what called you here, what you are carrying, or simply that you passed through. The thread builds slowly, like a trail of cairns. Someone who arrives after you will find it.

There is no algorithm here. No feed to scroll. Only the quiet accumulation of people who found the same road and stopped long enough to say so.

"The land holds memory. You only have to walk slowly enough to hear it."

Comments are read before they appear. This is not a moderation wall — it is the same care you would give a guestbook left open on a table. Most things pass through. Some are returned quietly.

Replies are threaded, so a conversation can form around a single thought. A good comment here is worth more than a hundred somewhere else.
