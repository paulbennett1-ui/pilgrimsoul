Sacred Sites & Landscape

10 links
The Megalithic Portal
The definitive crowd-sourced database of prehistoric sites across Britain and Ireland. Searchable, mapped, extraordinary.
megalithic.co.uk
The Modern Antiquarian
Julian Cope's vast, opinionated, deeply felt atlas of megalithic Britain. Thousands of sites with photographs, accounts and atmosphere.
themodernantiquarian.com
Ancient Origins
Archaeology, ancient history and sacred sites from around the world. Serious depth, regularly updated.
ancient-origins.net
Sacred Sites — Martin Gray
Global pilgrimage photography project. Visually stunning, spiritually rich. A life's work in images.
sacredsites.com
Heritage Calling
Historic England's own blog. Accessible guides to prehistoric monuments and lesser-known ancient sites.
heritagecalling.com
Sacred Sites UK
Guided tours and deep knowledge of Britain's sacred landscape. Well chosen routes and genuine reverence.
sacredsitesuk.com
Holy Wells of Britain
The definitive guide to Britain's sacred springs and holy wells. Hundreds of sites mapped and described with care.
holywell.org.uk
Avebury.net
Comprehensive guide to the Avebury complex — the stones, the landscape, the archaeology, and the living calendar of events.
avebury.net
The Invisible Temple
Freddy Silva's deep research into sacred sites, ley alignments, ancient temples, and the encoded geometry of sacred landscape.
invisibletemple.com
English Heritage — Myths Map
An interactive map of English myth, legend and folklore tied to real places. Genuinely fascinating to explore.
mythsmap.english-heritage.org.uk

Need Help?

Good people who can help

If you've arrived here in a difficult moment, these are real organisations with real people. No judgment. No obligation. Just some doors worth knowing about.

Samaritans
Call 116 123, free, 24 hours a day, every day. Whatever you're going through.
samaritans.org
Mind — Crisis Support
Mental health information, helplines and crisis resources for England and Wales.
mind.org.uk
CALM
Call 0800 58 58 58, 5pm–midnight daily. Campaign Against Living Miserably. Webchat also available.
thecalmzone.net
Shout
Text SHOUT to 85258. Free, confidential, 24/7 crisis text support from real people.
giveusashout.org
Hub of Hope
Enter your postcode to find local mental health support anywhere in the UK. A genuinely useful tool.
hubofhope.co.uk
Shelter
Housing advice and emergency help. Call 0808 800 4444. Free, 8am–8pm weekdays.
shelter.org.uk
NHS 111
Call 111 and select option 2 for mental health crisis support. Free, 24/7, confidential.
111.nhs.uk
Citizens Advice
Free, independent advice on benefits, debt, housing and legal rights. There's always a route forward.
citizensadvice.org.uk
Andy's Man Club
Free peer support groups for men. Monday evenings, nationwide. No agenda. Just talk.
andysmanclub.co.uk
