The Guestbook

Every pilgrim's hostel has a guestbook. Every waymarked path has stones left by those who came before. This is that place.

Leave a word — where you are walking, what called you here, what you are carrying, or simply that you passed through. The thread builds slowly, like a trail of cairns. Someone who arrives after you will find it.

The campfire

Fellow Travellers — the server

There is also a Discord server — a place for those who want something more immediate than a comment thread. Live conversation, shared finds, questions asked into the open air.

It is new. It is quiet right now. That is honest, and it is not a reason to stay away — it is a reason to arrive early.

Every community like this begins with a handful of people sitting in an empty room, deciding that the room is worth staying in. If this calls to you, come and sit down.
