The Sessions

Music from the road.
Voices from the land.

Recorded in fields, by firesides, in pubs and on moors. Artists encountered along the way, and the pilgrim playing in the open air.

Passing Through

Artists of the road

Musicians, singers, and makers encountered on the journey. Recorded where they are — fireside, field, pub back room. Their music, their place, their moment.

The road is underway. The first sessions are already taking shape — three musicians in Somerset, and whoever else the journey brings. Watch this space.

Coming as the road unfolds
Hidden Gems

The extraordinary ones

Not every session earns a place here. This is for the ones that stopped time — where something truly magical happened. Elevated from Passing Through when the moment demands it.

These are rare. They cannot be planned or predicted — only recognised when they happen. The first Hidden Gem will know itself when it arrives.

Earned, not assigned
In the Moment

The pilgrim plays

Solo performances, collaborations, busking on a high street, a song on Dartmoor with no one watching. Unannounced, unpolished, alive.

These will arrive unannounced — a clip from a pub session, something recorded at a stone circle at dusk, a collaboration with someone met on the road that morning.
